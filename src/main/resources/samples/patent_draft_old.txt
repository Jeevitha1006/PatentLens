PATENT DRAFT: OLD SPECIFICATION (PRE-EXAMINATION)
TITLE: AUTONOMOUS UNMANNED AERIAL VEHICLE DISPATCH AND DELIVERY PLATFORM

CLAIMS:

1. A system for autonomous package delivery, comprising:
an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) having a plurality of propulsion rotors;
a global positioning system (GPS) receiver; and
a processor configured to receive delivery coordinates and navigate the UAV to the delivery coordinates to discharge a payload.

2. The system of claim 1, wherein the processor calculates an optimal flight trajectory using cloud-based weather telemetry.

3. The system of claim 1, further comprising an optical sensor array configured to identify ground landing obstacles.

4. The system of claim 3, wherein said optical sensor array causes the UAV to hover until said ground landing obstacle clears.

5. A computer-implemented method for delivering goods, comprising:
receiving, by a server, a purchase order comprising a customer destination;
transmitting the customer destination to an autonomous drone; and
directing the autonomous drone to fly toward the customer destination without human intervention.
