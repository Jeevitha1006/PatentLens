Stylometry Heuristics Viva Explanation:

This heuristic examines structural cadence rather than superficial word matching. Burstiness evaluates sentence-length variance: human patent drafters typically punctuate dense technical recitations with varied sentence constructions, yielding high variance (σ² > 50). In contrast, generative transformers naturally output text with normalized, uniform token lengths per clause. Type-Token Ratio (TTR) gauges lexical redundancy, and N-Gram rates pinpoint repetitive transitional phrases.
