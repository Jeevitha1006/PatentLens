Blockchain Viva Defense Notes:

Each block bundles: BlockIndex + Timestamp + PreviousHash + DocumentSHA256 + Nonce. Mining requires finding a nonce such that SHA-256(Block) begins with k leading zeros (Proof-of-Work). Modifying any document or block in historical storage alters its SHA-256 hash, causing the next block's previousHash pointer to mismatch. The Verify Integrity routine recomputes all hashes from Block 0 to N to guarantee mathematical immutability.
