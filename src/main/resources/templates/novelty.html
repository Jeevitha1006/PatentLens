Algorithm Explanation & Viva Notes
1. Term Frequency (TF)

TF(t, d) = count(t, d) / |d|
Measures the relative density of term t within individual claim d, normalized against claim word count to prevent bias toward longer claims.

2. Inverse Doc Frequency (IDF)

IDF(t) = ln((1+|D|) / (1+DF(t))) + 1
Penalizes common patent boilerplate terms ("comprising", "system") while boosting distinctive technical limitation words ("octree", "pneumatic").

3. Cosine Similarity Vector

Cosine = (A . B) / (||A|| * ||B||)
Computes angular directional overlap between claim vector and prior-art vector in Euclidean space. Novelty score is computed as (1 - Cosine) * 100.
